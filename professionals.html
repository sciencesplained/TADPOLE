Information for hospital teams

This page provides a brief overview for clinicians and research staff involved in TADPOLE. It is not a substitute for the full protocol, Investigator Site File or sponsor guidance.

Trial summary

  • Design: Multicentre pragmatic phase III randomised controlled trial with an internal pilot, embedded qualitative work, surgical and radiotherapy QA, and a recruitment SWAT.
  • Population: Adults with unilateral early breast cancer and low-volume axillary nodal disease (≤2 nodes involved on ultrasound, biopsy-proven), having primary surgery.
  • Interventions: Targeted axillary dissection (TAD) versus axillary node clearance (ANC), 2:1 randomisation.
  • Co-primary outcomes: Lymphoedema at 12 months and locoregional recurrence at 5 years.
  • Follow-up: Up to 60 months post-last axillary surgery, with trial-based economic evaluation.
